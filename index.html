Selected work

ADECEES / Diffusion-based anomaly detection for satellite imagery

An ensemble segmentation framework for real-time detection of CO2 point-source anomalies in Sentinel-3 multispectral imagery, built end to end from data ingestion through localisation and evaluation.

Built at ESA Φ-lab; accepted at NeurIPS 2025. Paper

Global CO2 super-resolution dataset Dataset

A global, daily, gap-free dataset of column-averaged CO2 (XCO2), produced by a CNN super-resolution model that raises the resolution of OCO-2 satellite data 16×, to about 3 km.

Developed and released during the PhD at Imperial College London; published in Remote Sensing, 2025.

3D gaze & multi-person tracking Code & Thesis

A 3D gaze-direction predictor combining face orientation and body posture estimation, paired with a multi-person tracking model robust to viewpoint changes in semi-open immersive environments.

MSc research, Imperial College London, 2019.
